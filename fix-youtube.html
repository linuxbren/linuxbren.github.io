Fix YouTube

Inspired by Omarchy, developed on Omarchy. We can fix ANYTHING.

YouTube won’t stay in theater mode, and then the comments are right in your face. This is a small free Chrome extension that keeps the wide player, and can also handle captions and autoplay.

free Chrome / Chromium MIT no tracking
Install it GitHub Privacy
what it does

Always theater mode

Keep the wide player, so the comments stay out of the way. Or just hide the theater button.

Captions your way

Force captions on or off, or hide the CC button.

Turn off autoplay for good

Force the next-up toggle off, or just hide the button.

For each one you can leave it alone, force it on, force it off, or hide the button. Pick something in the popup and it applies on the current tab, no reload. It also reapplies when you go to the next video.

install

Load it unpacked, for now

It’s not on the Chrome Web Store yet — I just submitted it. Until that’s up, load it unpacked in Chrome, Edge, Brave, or any Chromium browser:

  1. Download or clone the repo.
  2. Open chrome://extensions in the address bar.
  3. Turn on Developer mode.
  4. Click Load unpacked and pick the folder you just got.
git clone https://github.com/linuxbren/fix-youtube.git

That’s it. Open YouTube, click the icon, and set theater mode, captions, or autoplay.

No tracking, and settings stay in your browser. MIT licensed. Privacy policy. Not affiliated with YouTube or Google.
