Run AI models on your own Macs, together.

Caravan splits big models across two Macs, tunes itself for speed, and gives you a simple chat page you can open from anywhere. Open source, MLX and GGUF.

what it does

Caravan runs on your own Macs, not a cloud AI service. On the 1st Mac you get a chat page in your browser. If a model is too big for one Mac, Caravan splits it across a 2nd Mac so the two can share the work.

You can use Apple’s MLX models or GGUF models. Open Models, pick one that fits, click Download, then Load and chat. No model is included. Some publishers ask you to accept their terms first.

Two Macs, one model

Big models can run across two Macs. The 1st Mac is the one you talk to; the 2nd Mac helps with the load.

A simple chat page

Open it in your browser. Themes include Amber, Green, Meadow, and Campfire — the look of this page.

Tunes itself

Self-tuning learns from your chats. Auto-tune tries settings on the loaded model and keeps the fastest. You can watch it work, step by step.

A fair race

caravan bench compares two versions of the same model on short questions and long ones, then puts your usual model back. After Auto-tune, one version started answering sooner on short questions (0.28 s vs 0.46 s), and the other wrote 22% faster on long prompts.

From nearby, or away from home

Reach the chat page through Tailscale, or turn on access from other devices on your local network. Don’t put the page on the public internet.

Keeps models safe

A memory and garble check watches for bad output and settings that don’t actually help. Download progress shows up right under the model you clicked.

Updates every Mac

Caravan checks for a new version once a day. Click Update now on the 1st Mac and it updates the 2nd Mac first, then itself. A flickering Updating… button shows each step, and the page reloads itself when it’s done.

Under Advanced, a GGUF model can also use Prompt lookup and Guess-ahead. Guess-ahead only turns on if a smaller model of the same kind is already downloaded; Caravan will not download one for you.

new in day 27
  • Guesses kept. When a reply used Prompt lookup or Guess-ahead, the line under it ends with how many guessed words were kept, for example “guesses kept 88%”.
  • Faster GGUF writing. On the 1st Mac, Qwen2.5 Coder 7B GGUF with a small 0.5B helper wrote at 29.9 tok/s on short chats (+34%) and 42.9 on code edits (+93%), ahead of MLX at 23.4. Auto-tune measured +25% and kept Guess-ahead.
  • A helper only if you say yes. If a known small helper isn’t on this Mac, Settings offers it in one line under the model. Nothing downloads without a yes.
  • caravan bench --speedups measures Off, Prompt lookup, Guess-ahead, and Auto on short chats, code edits, and long prompts.
  • Clearer Auto-tune. Cancel shows Cancelling…, then a plain cancelled line. Applying a setting while it runs offers one “Cancel Auto-tune and apply” action. Buttons show a busy word and a result.
  • One-Mac Topology. With only one live Mac, Balance and Thunderbolt are hidden. A Mac whose Caravan is off shows as offline, with a grey light.

A new version can take about 5 minutes to show up after it’s published. The full Day 27 notes and the changelog are in the repo.

install

A few steps, on your Mac

You need a Mac with an Apple Silicon chip (M1 or newer), running macOS 14 or newer. Open Terminal (find it with Spotlight), paste this, and press Return:

(set -o pipefail; curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/linuxbren/caravan/caravan-v3-mlx-split/install | bash)

Setup checks your Mac, prepares what Caravan needs, and opens your browser. Open a new Terminal window afterward to use caravan status. You do not need Homebrew, Git, an administrator password, or developer tools. The command downloads and runs a script; read it first if you prefer. Downloads need internet access.

Caravan starts when you log in. After a reboot, unlock your Mac and log in. It brings back the last model you successfully loaded. Choose Unload first if you do not want that model to come back.

To add a 2nd Mac, run the same install command there, then use Topology → Add a Mac on both. Full install help is in the repo.
